How Silenzio works

From muscle activity to language.

A lightweight surface-EMG interface senses subtle facial and neck activity. A neural decoder maps those patterns into speech without requiring an audible voice.

01 / SENSE

Capture

Non-invasive electrodes record the electrical activity generated by the muscles involved in articulation.

02 / DECODE

Decode

Machine-learning models learn the temporal structure of silent articulation and map EMG patterns into linguistic representations.

03 / COMMUNICATE
OUT

Speak

Decoded language can appear as text, control a device, or be synthesized as speech — while the user remains silent.

A new interface for human expression.

Non-invasive first

02

Language, not gestures

The goal is not just a small vocabulary of commands. We are building toward fluid silent communication that preserves the structure of natural speech.
